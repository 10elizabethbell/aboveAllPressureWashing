# Design System: Above All Pressure Washing & Shrink Wrapping

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Wall Mid-Wash"**

The top of the page, from the logo down to the first water seam, is one dirty vinyl-siding wall. A mildew film sits over everything on it: the logo, the headline and the buttons. The visitor's finger, or a click-and-drag on desktop, is the pressure-washer jet that cuts it clean. When nobody is touching it, the wand sweeps the wall on its own. Below the wall the page settles into the owner's logo colors: indigo-navy bands and clean-siding mist bands, joined by animated water-sheet seams. Proof comes as a real before/after slider, a grid of real work photos, and reviews.

The page is phone-first and loud. Headings are heavy 900-weight uppercase in a promo-flyer voice, body copy is sentence case, and the pill buttons are sized for thumbs. Motion always comes from the materials (water, jet, droplets, siding), runs on one shared animation loop that pauses off screen, and holds a still frame under reduced motion.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Logo-derived indigo-navy and sky blue. Navy replaces black everywhere.
- The signature is a hero wall of CSS lap boards with a grime canvas multiplied over the live copy.
- Mist and navy bands alternate below the wall, and each change of band goes through a three-line water seam.
- One system font stack, set at 900 uppercase for headings. This is the owner's house style.
- Pills for everything tappable or tag-like, 18px corners for photos and cards, circles for handles.
- Soft shadows with negative spread: navy-tinted on mist, black-alpha only over navy or photos.

## Colors

A two-hue logo palette (indigo-navy for weight, sky blue for water) on cool mist grounds, plus four siding greys that exist only in the hero wall.

### Primary
- **Wordmark Indigo** (navy): ground of the services band, area band and footer; primary button fill; heading color on light grounds; the "% clean" score pill. This is the page's black.
- **Pressed Indigo** (navy-2): primary button hover only.

### Secondary
- **Swoosh Sky** (sky): water. Service-row arrow circles, the hose line in the steps, the middle seam stroke, focus rings, the sky period after navy headings on navy, the reviews scrollbar, the wash-hint hand icon. Use it for decoration or large type only.
- **Spray Sky** (sky-2): light water. Text selection, the "&" in the area headline, the slider jet's highlight.
- **Sky Ink** (sky-ink): the sky hue darkened to pass contrast on mist. Use it wherever sky has to be read on a light ground: heading periods on mist, hero meta icons, the Facebook icon.

### Neutral
- **Ink** (ink): body text on light grounds and in review cards. At 82% it fills the "Before" photo tag.
- **Soft Ink** (ink-soft): ledes, step copy, review source lines, the swipe note.
- **Clean Siding Mist** (mist): page background and light bands, the top-bar Call pill, hover fill for white buttons.
- **Deep Mist** (mist-2): fill behind photos while they load.
- **Soft Lilac** (on-navy-soft): secondary text on navy (ledes, service descriptions and pills, footer).
- **White** (white): buttons on navy, photo frame borders (6px), review cards, step dots, slider knob, Facebook pill, the "After" tag. Written as literal `#fff` in the CSS.

### Siding (hero wall only)
- **Board Light** (siding-light), **Board Face** (siding-face), **Board Shade** (siding-shade), **Board Seam** (siding-seam): the four stops of the wall's repeating lap-board gradient, from the top of each board down to its 1px seam line. Board Light also tints the glow behind the hero pitch line. These belong to the wall. Don't use them as general greys.

### Named Rules
**The Never-Black Rule.** Dark means Wordmark Indigo. No `#000` grounds or text. Black-alpha shadows are allowed only over navy (the service arrows, the white button on navy), where a navy tint wouldn't show.

**The Sky-Ink Rule.** Sky blue on a light ground is decoration. If it carries words or a meaningful icon, use sky-ink.

**The Neutral-Pill Rule.** Pills on navy are white at 7% with soft-lilac text, never sky. Sky-on-navy pills vibrated when many sat together.

## Typography

**Display Font:** system UI stack (`--font`)
**Body Font:** the same stack

**Character:** One family. Weight and case do all the work: 900 uppercase headings with 0.95 leading and -0.02em tracking read like the owner's flyers, set against relaxed 17px / 1.55 sentence-case body. The system stack is Ellie's confirmed house style, not a fallback.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, clamp 2.55rem to 5.6rem, 0.95): the hero headline only, with each period in sky-ink.
- **Headline** (900, clamp 2rem to 3.6rem, 0.95): section h2s. Its siblings are the area headline (clamp 2.2rem to 4.4rem), the promise line (clamp 1.8rem to 3rem, 1.05 leading) and the close heading (clamp 1.7rem to 3rem). Each ends in a sky or sky-ink period where the copy has one.
- **Title** (900): reviews heading (clamp 1.5rem to 2.2rem), service-row titles (clamp 1.35rem to 1.9rem), step titles (clamp 1.25rem to 1.6rem).
- **Pitch** (700, clamp 16px to 20px): the single line under the hero headline, in navy.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.55): ledes (60ch max), review quotes, step copy (52ch). Service descriptions use 16px.
- **UI sizes:** buttons 17px / 800 / 0.01em; top-bar Call, Facebook pill and footer 15px; hero meta row, wash hint and swipe note 14px; service pills (600), wash score (800, tabular numbers) and review sources (600) 13px; photo tags 12px / 800 uppercase / 0.05em.

### Named Rules
**The Shout-and-Explain Rule.** All h1 to h3 (and the two display-styled paragraphs in the area band) are 900 uppercase with tight leading and `text-wrap: balance`. Everything else is sentence case with `text-wrap: pretty`.

**The Closed-Ramp Rule.** Fixed UI text uses only the 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 17px steps. Fluid sizes are clamp() roles from the frontmatter. Don't add a new size between them.

## Layout

One centred container (1180px max, 16px gutters, 32px from 760px) inside full-bleed bands. Sections pad 44px top / 52px bottom on phones and 64 / 76px from 760px. The band order is the wall (siding), navy services, mist work and reviews, mist "How a job goes", navy area, mist close, navy footer. Every change between navy and mist goes through a seam.

**Breakpoints.** **760px:** wider gutters, the hero Call and Text buttons sit side by side, seams grow to 64px, wall boards to 56px, reviews scroll at two-up. Below 760px the small gallery tiles drop their "Shrink wrap ·" tag prefix. **1000px:** desktop. The top bar grows to 84px with a 62px logo, hero copy caps at 780px, and the wash hint and score move to the right edge of the container. Each service group becomes a two-column grid (1fr : 1.25fr, 56px gap) with its heading sticky at 32px. The work grid becomes the slider (0.8fr) beside the photo grid (1fr) at 16px gaps. Reviews become a 3 + 2 grid, and the "How a job goes" and area sections become two-column grids with 56px gaps. `.mob` and `.desk` swap phone and desktop wording; desktop buttons show the phone number.

**The Thumb-Reach Rule.** Every tap target is at least 44px. Primary actions are 58px pills, stacked full width on phones.

**The No-Bottom-Bar Rule.** Nothing is fixed or sticky to the bottom of the phone viewport. Contact lives in the top-bar Call pill, the hero buttons, every service row and the closing buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is soft and ambient. Every shadow has a large blur and a negative spread, so things sit just above the ground without hard edges. On mist the tint is navy; over navy, and for handles over photos or the wall, it is a darker indigo or black alpha. Photo frames get their depth from a 6px white border plus the shared frame shadow. The full shadow vocabulary is in the sidecar.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Frame lift** (`--shadow`, `0 10px 30px -12px rgba(38,33,94,.35)`): the slider, gallery tiles and the Facebook pill.
- **Button lift** (`0 8px 20px -8px rgba(38,33,94,.6)`): primary button on mist. On navy it is `0 8px 22px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`.
- **Card rest** (`0 8px 24px -16px rgba(38,33,94,.45)`): review cards.
- **Dot** (`0 6px 14px -6px rgba(38,33,94,.5)`): step dots.
- **Arrow on navy** (`0 6px 16px -6px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`): service-row arrow circles.
- **Wall handle** (`0 6px 16px -6px rgba(20,20,60,.45)`): the wash-hint pill floating on the wall.
- **Knob** (`0 6px 16px -4px rgba(20,20,60,.5)`): the slider knob over photos.

**The Lift-Not-Grow Rule.** Hover lifts a button by `translateY(-2px)` and shifts its fill. Shadows never grow on hover.

## Shapes

Three corner levels. **Pills** (999px) for everything tapped or read as a tag: buttons, the top-bar Call pill, service pills, photo tags, the Facebook link, the wash hint and score. **Cards** (18px, `--radius`) for the slider, gallery tiles and review cards. **Focus** (6px) for focus rings, which are a 3px sky outline at 3px offset; the hose line has the same 6px rounding. Circles for the service arrow (44px), step dots and slider knob (48px).

Lines are water. Seams are three wavy strokes (pale sky, sky, white). The steps hang off a 6px sky hose line, and the slider divider is a 4px white-to-spray-sky jet. On navy, service rows are separated by 1px white hairlines at 18%.

## Components

### Buttons
Big confident pills that always come as a pair: Call (primary) and Text (secondary), same height, same 2px outline.
- **Shape:** pill, 58px min height, 0 22px padding, 17px / 800 label with a 22px stroke icon.
- **On mist:** primary is navy fill and white text with button lift; secondary is white fill and navy text. Both have a 2px navy border.
- **On navy:** primary turns white with navy text; secondary turns transparent with white text and a white border.
- **States:** on hover devices only, hover lifts 2px and shifts the fill (navy-2, mist, or white at 10% on navy). Active scales to 0.97. Transitions are `.2s cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`.
- **Top-bar Call:** a smaller 44px mist pill, navy 15px / 800, reading "Call" on phones and the number on desktop.

### Service rows
Full-row text links on navy. Each row has a 900 uppercase title, a strip of neutral pills (white at 7%, soft lilac, 13px / 600, 4px 10px), and a 44px sky arrow circle on the right with a white arrow. On hover (or press on touch) the circle turns white with a navy arrow and slides 4px right. Rows are split by 1px white hairlines at 18%. Every row opens a pre-filled text that names the service.

### Work grid
One grid. On phones the siding before/after slider comes first, then the photos. On desktop the slider is the tall left tile, stretched to the photos' height. The photos are a two-column grid with one 16:9 wide tile on top and two square tiles under it. Every tile has 18px corners, a 6px white border, frame lift, and a deep-mist fill while loading. Photo tags are pinned top-left ("Before · …" and work labels, in ink at 82% with white text) and top-right ("After", white with navy text).
- **Slider:** the after image clips at `--pos`. The divider is a 4px jet and the knob a 48px white circle with a double chevron. It takes pointer drag, horizontal-only touch drag, arrow keys (5% steps) and Home/End. It exposes `role="slider"` with value text.
- **Facebook pill:** a white pill under the grid (15px / 800 navy, sky-ink Facebook icon, frame lift) whose trailing arrow slides 4px right on hover.

### Reviews
White cards with 18px corners, card-rest shadow and 20px padding. The quote is 17px / 1.5 with hanging punctuation, and the name is navy 800 pinned to the bottom over a 13px soft-ink source line. On phones they sit in a snap-scroll row (86% wide, two-up at 760px) with a "Swipe for more" note. On desktop they become a 3 + 2 grid.

### Steps ("How a job goes")
One hose, three stops. A 6px sky line runs down the left. Each stop is a 48px white circle (dot shadow) with a navy 24px stroke icon, a step title and soft-ink copy indented 66px.

### Seams (water-sheet dividers)
SVG dividers, 44px tall (64px from 760px), drawn by the seams script. Three moving strokes (spray sky at 55%, sky, white at 70%), with the lower band's color filling under the middle stroke. The upper and lower colors are hex literals in each seam's `data-top` / `data-bottom`. The first seam closes the wall (transparent to navy).

### The wall (signature)
The header and hero share one `.wall` container. Its background is a repeating lap-board gradient (board light, board face from 70%, board shade, then a 1px board seam), with boards 40px tall on phones and 56px from 760px. Two canvases cover it, both ignoring the pointer so every link and button underneath still works. The **grime** canvas is low-res procedural mildew with rain streaks, darker under each board lip, blended with `multiply` so the copy under it stays readable. The **fx** canvas carries the fan-shaped white jet, a breathing impact mist and spray droplets. A wash-hint pill ("Drag to wash the wall") sits bottom-left on phones and fades out on first touch, and a navy "% clean" score pill fades in. The hero pitch line carries a soft board-light glow so a board seam doesn't cut through it.

### Motion
- **Wall wash:** the idle wand makes horizontal passes, slowly drifting up and down. A finger or click-drag takes over, and control returns to the wand 1800ms after release. Grime creeps back in patches and the wet sheen dries. Tunables live in `CFG`: `JET_RADIUS` 30 (0.85x on phones, 1.4x on the desktop wall), `REGROW` 0.0045, `AUTO_SPEED` 1.0, `IDLE_RETURN_MS` 1800, droplets 90 on phones and 240 on desktop. `overscroll-behavior-y: none` keeps a downward drag at the top on the wand instead of the page.
- **Seams:** three summed sine waves per stroke; `SEAM_SPEED` 1.0, `SEAM_AMP` 0.34.
- **Slider demo sweep:** the first time the slider is 60% in view it waits 400ms, then does one 1600ms back-and-forth of up to 22% that eases back to 50%. It stops the moment someone touches it.
- **Image fade-in:** lazy photos start at opacity 0 and fade in over `.5s ease-out`, so a broken-image icon never shows.
- **UI:** buttons `.2s`, arrows `.25s` on `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`; wash hint `.6s` fade and slide, score `.4s` fade.
- **Discipline:** one shared requestAnimationFrame loop. The wall and seams pause off screen (IntersectionObserver, 80px margin). Under reduced motion all transitions are off, the wall shows a still frame with one clean stripe, the seams are static and the slider demo is skipped.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every color from the frontmatter tokens: navy for anything dark, sky-ink for sky that carries meaning on mist, the siding greys only inside the wall.
- **Do** alternate mist and navy bands and join every change with a seam.
- **Do** set headings in 900 uppercase at 0.95 leading, and keep body copy sentence case at 17px / 1.55.
- **Do** use pills for tappable things and tags, 18px corners for photos and cards, and circles for handles.
- **Do** keep Call and Text as a matched pair (same height, same border) everywhere they appear.
- **Do** run new motion through the shared loop, pause it off screen, give it a reduced-motion still frame, and build it from the world's materials (water, jet, droplets, siding).
- **Do** write all page copy in first person as the owner, Sean ("I", "me", "my"). Customer testimonials and the pre-filled SMS bodies are the only exceptions.
- **Do** use the house-and-wand mark cropped from the logo as the favicon and home-screen icon. Never use the full wordmark at tab size.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use black grounds or black text (The Never-Black Rule).
- **Don't** load a webfont. The system stack is the owner's house style.
- **Don't** use the logo's wand red as a UI color. It belongs to the logo image.
- **Don't** put small uppercase tracked labels (eyebrows or kickers) above headings. The uppercase 12px style belongs only to photo tags that sit on images.
- **Don't** put a sticky or fixed bar at the bottom of the screen on phones (The No-Bottom-Bar Rule).
- **Don't** put email buttons or email links anywhere except the footer.
- **Don't** add "Demo one-pager", "free sample" or any other pitch-meta text to the page.
- **Don't** use hard-offset shadows, or black shadows on mist.
- **Don't** add a second animation loop or per-element requestAnimationFrame.

## Where to edit

| Want to change | Where |
|---|---|
| Brand colors, card radius, frame shadow, font stack | `:root` at the top of `<style>` |
| Heading weight, case, leading | the global `h1,h2,h3` rule |
| Individual heading sizes | each component's selector (`.hero h1`, `.sec h2`, `.svc h3`, `.area-big`, `.area-line`, `.close h2`) |
| Wall boards and siding colors | `.wall` (`--board`, `--seam-h`, the repeating gradient); grime colors in `build()` of the wash script |
| Section padding, gutters, breakpoints | `.sec`, `.wrap`, the `760px` / `1000px` media queries |
| Jet size, regrowth, idle sweep, droplets, seam speed and swell | `CFG` at the top of the main `<script>` |
| Seam colors | `data-top` / `data-bottom` on each seam, plus the stroke colors in the seams script |
| Phone number, business name | `CFG.PHONE`, `CFG.BUSINESS`, plus the `tel:` / `sms:` hrefs |
